# Residential Lease Agreement (sample)

This document is a made-up example for trying Mehteran. The people and addresses are not real.

**Landlord:** Anna Becker
**Tenant:** Tom Hughes
**Property:** 12 Elm Street, Flat 4, Bristol
**Start:** 1 March 2026 · **Term:** 1 year

## Clause 1: Rent

The monthly rent is £1,200, payable to the landlord's bank account by the 5th of each month. If a payment is more than 10 days late, the tenant pays a late fee of 0.5% of the monthly rent for each day of delay.

## Clause 2: Deposit

On signing, the tenant pays a deposit equal to two months' rent (£2,400). The deposit is returned within 30 days after the property is handed back undamaged and with no money owed.

## Clause 3: Rent increase

When the agreement is renewed, the rent rises by the average consumer price index (CPI) of the previous 12 months.

## Clause 4: Maintenance and repairs

Small repairs that come from everyday use (taps, light bulbs, door handles) are the tenant's. Large repairs to the building, such as the roof, the pipes or the boiler, are the landlord's. If the tenant does not report a fault in writing within 7 days, the tenant is liable for the extra damage that the late report causes.

## Clause 5: Subletting and pets

The tenant may not sublet the property without the landlord's written consent. Keeping pets needs the landlord's written consent.

## Clause 6: End of the agreement

Unless the tenant gives written notice at least 15 days before the term ends, the agreement renews for one more year on the same terms. If the tenant wants to leave before the term ends, the tenant pays compensation equal to two months' rent.
